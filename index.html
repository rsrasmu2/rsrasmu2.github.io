Personal Project

Open-World Game in Bevy

An in-progress 3D open-world game built in Bevy (Rust), with an HD-2D visual style and a hexagonal world structure. It doubles as a sandbox for hand-written real-time rendering: WGSL shaders for volumetric fog, interactive water and grass, and cloud shadows over voxel terrain, on top of a bindless terrain material pipeline — plus a custom Blender-to-Bevy asset workflow and tooling.
